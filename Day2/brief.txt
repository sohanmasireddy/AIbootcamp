An AI-powered notepad built for both writing and coding.

Most note-taking apps are designed primarily for text, while coding tools are built mainly for developers. People who write documentation, take technical notes, or learn programming often have to switch between multiple applications.

This app combines both in one fast, lightweight workspace. Users can instantly switch between a distraction-free text mode and a richer graphical editing mode. Built-in AI helps by fixing grammar and spelling in written notes while also debugging, explaining, and improving code. Instead of juggling separate apps for writing and programming, users can do both in one place.
